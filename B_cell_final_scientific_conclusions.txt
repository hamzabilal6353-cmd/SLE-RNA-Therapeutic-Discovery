SLE B-CELL THERAPEUTIC TARGET DISCOVERY

MAIN FINDINGS
1. Original analysis identified 1586 significant genes.
2. Interferon-related biological processes were enriched.
3. Six candidate genes were investigated:
   TLR7, USP18, JAK1, JAK2, IFIH1, STAT1.
4. Filtering samples with fewer than 100 B cells
   retained 259 samples.
5. All six candidates remained significant (FDR < 0.05).
6. All six retained their expression directions.
7. Maximum absolute logFC change was 0.0446.

LIMITATIONS
- Sensitivity analysis used the same dataset.
- Independent-cohort validation has not been performed.
- Protein activity and therapeutic efficacy are unverified.
- RNA expression does not establish intervention direction.
- Flare versus Managed comparisons are batch-confounded.

CONCLUSION
Interferon-associated B-cell expression changes in SLE
support further investigation of the six candidates.
Functional and independent validation are required.
